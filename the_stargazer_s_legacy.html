COMPENDIUM OF THE STARGAZER // ADVENTURER'S MANUAL

Complete Guide to Ancestries, Classes, Attributes, Combat, Loot & Progression

1. CORE ATTRIBUTES

STR (Strength): Melee to-hit and damage modifier ((score − 10) ÷ 2, as in classic D&D). Heavy weapons use it.

DEX (Dexterity): Adds to Armor Class (limited by heavy armor), to bows and finesse weapons (daggers, Astral Blade), and to your odds of fleeing.

INT (Intelligence): Powers Arcane spells: the saving-throw DC and damage of Arcane spells, and extra mana for Wizards & Rangers.

WIS (Wisdom): Powers divine spells (Heal, Harm, Resurrect...) and adds mana for Clerics, Paladins & Rangers. Warriors have no mana at all.

2. LEVELING UP, STAMINA & TIME

Spell Progression: Every spellcaster starts with two spells. Cleric & Wizard learn the rest as they level up (Lv 2, 3, 4 …); Paladins & Rangers learn them 2 levels later. Open [C] to see locked spells and the level that unlocks them.

Ships: Deep water cannot be crossed on foot. Buy a Ship Deed (400 gold) from the harbor master of any port town, name thy ship, and she moors beside that port. Stand next to her (or walk into her) and press [X] to board; press [X] beside a shore to disembark. The harbor master can also tow her to another harbor or repaint her name. Surf is audible along every coast.

Ultima VI Magic: Spells use mana only - no reagents. Status and death spells (Sleep, Poison, Paralyze, Charm, Kill, Mass Kill, Death Wind…) let the foe make a saving throw against your spell DC (8 + proficiency + INT/WIS); dragons and liches save with a bonus. Summons (Conjure, Insect Swarm, Summon) fight beside you for a few rounds. Fields (Fire, Poison, Sleep, Energy) burn, poison, lull or wall off a column of tiles. Time Stop freezes the foes for two rounds. Armageddon (Wizard Lv 20, 120 MP) destroys every foe in a battle — once per rest. Light, Create Food, Locate, Peer and Gate Travel work outside battle.

Choosing Spell Targets: Aimed spells (Magic Arrow, Sleep, Fireball, Lightning, Kill, the Winds, Chain Bolt, the Fields…) and helping spells (Heal, Cure, Protection, Resurrect) ask whom to target when there is a choice. Move the reticle with arrows / WASD, cycle with [Tab] or click a target, then press [Enter] to cast; [Esc] cancels without spending mana. Blasts, winds and chains preview every foe they will catch.

Marshes, Rivers & Bridges: Rivers follow their real courses: each rises at a spring in the mountains and runs to the sea (or into a larger river - the Lahn flows past Castle Stargazer into the Rhine). Rivers are too deep to cross and too shallow for ships; cross them on the bridges built near every town, castle, village and dungeon and at intervals along long rivers. Marsh steps cost 2 stamina and may poison heroes without Swamp Boots (sold by every smith, 35 gold a pair). Poison drains 1 HP per step (never below 1) until a healer, an inn bed, a night in camp or the Cleric spell Cure purges it.

Weather: Rain and thunderstorms roll in as time passes outdoors. Rain shortens sight by 1 tile and gives -1 to hit (missiles -2) in open-air battles; a thunderstorm shortens sight by 2 and gives -2 (missiles -3) — for heroes and monsters alike. Spells are unaffected. Dungeons stay dry.

Magic Items: Chests (and every slain mimic) may hold enchanted gear with +1 to +5 to hit & damage (weapons) or AC (armour), plus affixes — prefixes such as Flaming, Shocking, Keen, Holy, Vicious, Warding, Thorned, Marshwalker and suffixes such as of the Leech, of Speed, of Fortune, of Life, of Regeneration, of the Bear. Blue names are magic, gold names are rare (prefix and suffix). Deeper dungeons and stronger parties find better loot. Equip them in [I], sell them to a smith.

Fleeing: In combat press [F] to try to flee with the active hero (better odds with high DEX, near the left edge and with few awake foes). Badly wounded monsters may flee too — they give no XP once gone.

Zoom & Music: Zoom the overland map, towns and battles with the mouse wheel, [+] / [-] or the buttons in the corner of the view (75% - 300%). Each region has its own theme - overland, sea, town and dungeon - and battles have a driving combat theme; toggle music with [N].

Look / Inspect: Press [L] (or just click a tile) to examine things without spending time. Move the cursor with WASD / arrows, jump between interesting targets with [T], and close it with [L] or [ESC]. It reveals monster HP, damage, reach, rewards and how dangerous a pack is; names townsfolk, guards, pets and furniture; shows terrain costs; and in dungeons lets you look ahead and aside. In combat it also tells you what a foe would do to the active hero and whether it is in reach.

Leveling Up (D&D style): Vanquishing monsters grants XP. At Level × 100 XP visit Lord Stargazer or any Local Lord and select TRAIN. Each level rolls your class hit die for HP (Fighter/Paladin/Ranger d10, Rogue/Cleric d8, Wizard d6; never less than half the die) plus +2 hardiness; Dwarves gain +1 more, Sylphs −1. Casters gain mana, and every even level raises the two prime attributes by +1. Level cap: 20.

Combat Rules: Attacks roll a d20 + proficiency + ability modifier against the target's Armor Class. A natural 20 is a critical hit (double dice); a natural 1 always misses. Damage uses weapon dice plus your ability modifier. Spell damage is rolled in dice and never misses. Sleeping foes are hit automatically. Proficiency starts at +2 and rises every 4 levels.

Inns, Camping & Night: Innkeepers rent a bed for 2 gold (speak ROOM): the living are restored to full HP and MP and morning comes. Camping [R] only recovers 50% HP and MP. Towns, villages and castles have a day/night cycle — lamps, hearths and windows light up after dusk.

Stamina & Terrain Fatigue: Every step in the wild advances the clock by 1 hour (inside towns time stands still - only sleeping at an inn moves it) and consumes Stamina (1% on Grasslands, 2% in Forests, 3% over Mountains). At 0 Stamina, exhaustion inflicts 1 HP damage per step. Press [R] to pitch camp for 8 hours and restore Stamina to 100%.

Line of Sight & Moongates: Daylight grants 7-tile sight radius, while Night reduces vision to 2 tiles. The 8 Moongates teleport you based on the twin moons Astra & Luna.

3. FIVE ANCESTRIES

  • Human: All +0 — Versatile champions suited to any calling.
  • Elf: STR −1, DEX +2, INT +1 — Keen-eyed forestfolk; premier Rangers and Wizards.
  • Dwarf: STR +2, DEX −1, INT −1, WIS +1, +1 HP/level — Tireless mountain warriors.
  • Halfling: STR −2, DEX +2, WIS +1 — Nimble, lucky scouts who excel as Rogues and Clerics.
  • Sylph: STR −2, DEX +1, INT +2, WIS +1, −1 HP/level — Frail star-born mystics with deep mana reserves.

4. SIX HEROIC CLASSES

  • ⚔ Fighter: d10 HP | No mana | Masters of all weapons & heavy Plate/Astral armor.
  • 🛡 Paladin: d10 HP | Divine magic (low mana) | Holy knights able to wear Plate and cast heals.
  • 🏹 Ranger: d10 HP | Hybrid magic (low mana) | Bows, Broadswords, Chainmail & both spell schools.
  • 🗡 Rogue: d8 HP | No mana | High-DEX skirmisher using Bows, Maces & Studded Armor.
  • ✚ Cleric: d8 HP | Divine magic (good mana) | Healer, protector & undead-repeller; wears up to Chainmail.
  • ✦ Wizard: d6 HP | Arcane magic (deep mana) | Daggers & Cloth only, but commands fire, lightning, summons and finally Armageddon.
📦 MONSTER CHEST DROPS, HEALING POTIONS & MIMICS: Slain monsters often leave behind a Treasure Chest! Opening a chest can reward Gold pouches, Healing Potions (2d6+4 HP, usable in [I] Inventory or [C] Combat), or rare Weapons & Armor — often magic (+1 to +5, with affixes like Flaming or of the Leech; gold names carry two) — but beware: some chests are ravenous Mimics in disguise that will ambush your party!
