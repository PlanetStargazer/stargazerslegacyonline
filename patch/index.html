COMPENDIUM OF THE STARGAZER // ADVENTURER'S MANUAL

Complete Guide to Ancestries, Classes, Attributes, Combat, Loot & Progression

1. CORE ATTRIBUTES

STR (Strength): Melee to-hit and damage modifier ((score − 10) ÷ 2). Heavy weapons (Broadswords, Halberds, Maces) rely on it.

DEX (Dexterity): Adds to Armor Class (capped by heavy armor), weapons of finesse (Daggers, Astral Blade), Hunting Bows and firearms, and boosts escape odds when fleeing.

INT (Intelligence): Governs Arcane spells: spell saving-throw DC, spell power, and bonus mana for Wizards, Rangers, Tinkers (gadgets & constructs) and, alongside WIS, Bards.

WIS (Wisdom): Governs divine prayers (Heal, Cure, Harm, Resurrect) and provides mana for Clerics, Paladins, Rangers, and Bards. Fighters and Rogues command no mana.

2. YOUR HERO & ADVANCEMENT

Champion Customization: Every adventurer controls a unique champion. Tailor gender, hair color, hair style and beard; equipped weapons, armor, headgear and shields appear in full vector detail on your avatar. Tailors and barbers in major settlements allow cosmetic changes.

Leveling Up: Every monster slain grants its full XP to every adventurer on the battlefield, and gold spoils are divided fairly. At Level × 100 XP visit Lord Stargazer or any settlement Lord to TRAIN. Each level rolls your class hit die for maximum HP (never less than half the die) plus +2 hardiness (+1 more for Dwarves and Automata). Spellcasters gain mana, and every even level grants +1 to your two primary attributes up to the maximum level of 20.

3. COMBAT & AUTO-ATTACK

D20 Combat System: Attacks roll d20 + proficiency bonus + attribute modifier against the target's total Armor Class. Rolling a natural 20 lands a critical strike for double damage dice; a natural 1 always misses. Sleeping foes are hit automatically. Proficiency begins at +2 and scales every 4 levels.

Targeting & Auto-Attack: Click any enemy on the battlefield to lock targeting (red targeting reticle). Press [Q] or the AUTO button to automatically attack that enemy each turn; if out of range, your hero waits until they close in. Auto-attack continues until the target falls or you press [Q] again. Walking into an enemy strikes them in melee.

Fleeing: Press [F] during battle to attempt escape. Heroes with high DEX, standing near the left border, or facing wounded or sleeping foes enjoy greater escape odds.

4. MAGIC & THE GRIMOIRE

Spellcasting: Press [C] to access spells and consumables. Spells expend mana only—no components required. Status effects and lethal spells allow targets a saving throw against your Spell DC (8 + proficiency + INT/WIS). Summons fight alongside you for multiple rounds, and elemental barrier fields cordon off corridors.

Target Selection: Aimed projectile spells and restorative prayers allow you to target specific tiles or companions using arrow keys, WASD, or direct clicking. Blast and cone spells highlight all targets in their area of effect.

5. TRAVEL, RIVERS & THE SEAS

Sailing & Ships: Broad oceans require sea vessels. Purchase a Ship Deed (400 gold) from any harbor master to commission your frigate. Board or disembark by pressing [X] adjacent to your ship or coastlines.

Rivers, Bridges & Swamps: Major waterways cannot be waded through on foot; seek stone bridges to cross safely. Marshes can cause deadly Poison that ticks down HP every second until cured by a healer, an inn rest, camping, or the Cleric's Cure spell. Equipping Swamp Boots grants total immunity to marsh poison.

Moongates & Vision: Daylight allows 7-tile vision; night restricts sight to 2 tiles. Eight mystic Moongates open portals keyed to the phases of the twin moons Astra & Luna.

6. TIME, CAMPING & INNS

The Living World Clock: Time marches forward continuously: 1 game hour equals 2 real minutes (a full game day spans 48 minutes). Towns and castles light lanterns at dusk.

Camping & Inns: Renting an inn bed (2 gold) or pitching camp in the wild ([R], expending 1 travel ration) allows HP and MP to regenerate steadily over time. Monsters give campfire embers a wide berth.

7. EQUIPMENT, SHIELDS & LOOT

16-Slot Backpack: Press [I] to inspect your paperdoll and arrange inventory. Equip weapons, armor, headgear, shields, swamp boots and pets by clicking or dragging. Right-click potions to drink.

Shields & Two-Handed Weapons: Shields grant direct AC bonuses and can be purchased from smiths, found in chests, or dropped by monsters. Two-handed weapons (Hunting Bow, Silver Halberd, Musket Rifle, Blunderbuss) require both hands and cannot be held alongside a shield.

Enchanted & Rare Gear: Defeating monsters or finding treasure chests can yield magical items (+1 to +5 with powerful prefixes and suffixes). Blue items possess one magical affix; gold items are rare treasures carrying both prefix and suffix.

THE FIVE ANCESTRIES OF ASTRA

Your ancestry shapes your foundational ability scores and grants unique inherent traits:

  • Human: All Attributes +0
    Versatile and ambitious champions adaptable to every calling and martial discipline.
  • Elf: STR −1, DEX +2, INT +1
    Keen-eyed woodland folk with unmatched grace, excelling as Rangers, Rogues, and Arcane Wizards.
  • Dwarf: STR +2, DEX −1, INT −1, WIS +1, +1 HP per level
    Stout, unyielding mountain dwellers renowned for legendary resilience as Fighters, Paladins, and Clerics.
  • Halfling: STR −2, DEX +2, WIS +1
    Nimble, fleet-footed scouts gifted with natural luck, quick reflexes, and keen devotion as Rogues, Bards, and Clerics.
  • Automaton: STR +1, DEX −1, +1 HP per level, +1 AC
    Ancient sentient beings of bronze, rune-stone, and clockwork. Their integrated chassis grants permanent +1 AC, and their inorganic bodies are completely immune to poison (marsh water, venom, and toxic vapors).

⚔ THE FIGHTER // CHAMPION OF STEEL

Hit Die: d10 • Mana: None • Difficulty: Beginner

Role & Strengths: The Fighter is the supreme master of physical combat and the sturdiest frontline warrior in the realm. With a massive d10 hit die and access to the heaviest plate armor and largest bulwarks, Fighters excel at absorbing furious enemy punishment while cutting down foes.

Primary Attributes:

  • Strength (STR): Maximizes to-hit accuracy and raw damage with melee weapons.
  • Dexterity (DEX): Boosts ranged bow attacks and supplementary defense.

Equipment Proficiencies:

  • Weapons: Proficient with all weapons: Dagger, Heavy Mace, Hunting Bow, Broadsword, Silver Halberd, Astral Blade.
  • Armors: Cloth, Leather, Studded Cuirass, Chainmail, Steel Plate, Astral Aegis.
  • Shields: Buckler (+1 AC), Round Shield (+2 AC), Heater Shield (+2 AC), Tower Shield (+3 AC), Astral Barrier (+4 AC).
Tactical Guidance:

Pairing a Broadsword or enchanted Astral Blade with a Tower Shield or Astral Barrier gives the highest Armor Class achievable in the game, rendering standard monster attacks nearly harmless. When facing clustered monsters or striking over water, equip the Silver Halberd for devastating 2-tile reach attacks (remembering that two-handed weapons require stowing your shield).

🛡 THE PALADIN // HOLY DEFENDER

Hit Die: d10 • Mana: Divine • Difficulty: Moderate

Role & Strengths: Paladins are noble champions who temper martial steel with sacred miracles. Boasting the formidable d10 hit die and heavy plate armor proficiencies of a warrior, they also command divine spells to heal wounds, protect companions, and banish undead horrors.

Primary Attributes:

  • Strength (STR): Powers weapon attacks and heavy martial strikes.
  • Wisdom (WIS): Determines divine spell potency and maximum mana pool.

Equipment Proficiencies:

  • Weapons: Broadswords, Heavy Maces, Astral Blades, Silver Halberds.
  • Armors: All armor tiers up to Steel Plate and Astral Aegis.
  • Shields: Buckler, Round Shield, Heater Shield, Tower Shield, Astral Barrier.
Sacred Prayers & Tactics:

Paladins learn divine prayers 2 levels after Clerics, starting with holy light and healing. While their mana pool is more modest than a Cleric's, the ability to self-heal while encased in plate mail with a heavy Heater or Tower Shield makes them premier solo survivors and indispensable anchors in cooperative group fights.

🏹 THE RANGER // WILDERNESS SNIPER

Hit Die: d10 • Mana: Hybrid • Difficulty: Moderate

Role & Strengths: Rangers are master trackers and archers of Astra's vast untamed wilds. They blend heavy martial vigor (d10 hit die) with deadly ranged marksmanship, nature lore, and hybrid spellcasting drawn from both arcane and divine traditions.

Primary Attributes:

  • Dexterity (DEX): Maximizes archery accuracy, bow damage, and evasive defense.
  • Wisdom & Intelligence: Expands hybrid mana capacity and spell efficacy.

Equipment Proficiencies:

  • Weapons: Hunting Bow (4-tile range), Daggers, Broadswords, Astral Blades.
  • Armors: Cloth, Leather Armor, Studded Cuirass, Chainmail Hauberk.
  • Shields: Buckler (+1 AC) and Round Shield (+2 AC) for close combat.
Archery & Hybrid Sorcery:

With high Dexterity, a Hunting Bow keeps you safely out of enemy melee reach while picking off threatening spellcasters. If enemies break through, switch to an Astral Blade and Round Shield to stand your ground, supported by utility spells like Heal, Magic Arrow, and Light.

🗡 THE ROGUE // SHADOW STRIKER

Hit Die: d8 • Mana: None • Difficulty: Moderate

Role & Strengths: Rogues live by stealth, lightning speed, and surgical precision. Unencumbered by cumbersome plate or spellbooks, they convert pure Dexterity into untouchable evasion, devastating critical strikes, and the highest flee chance in the game.

Primary Attributes:

  • Dexterity (DEX): Core stat providing massive Armor Class bonuses in light armor, maximum to-hit and damage with finesse daggers and bows, and effortless escapes.
  • Intelligence (INT): Enhances secondary skills and tactical perception.

Equipment Proficiencies:

  • Weapons: Daggers, Heavy Maces, Hunting Bows.
  • Armors: Cloth Tunic, Leather Armor, Studded Cuirass.
  • Shields: Agile Bucklers (+1 AC).
Precision & Skirmishing:

Because Studded Cuirass allows a generous DEX cap, high-level Rogues often match or surpass heavily armored warriors in dodge defense. Seek out weapons with the Keen affix (critical strikes on 19-20) or of the Leech to sustain your health bar indefinitely through rapid strikes.

✚ THE CLERIC // ANOINTED HEALER

Hit Die: d8 • Mana: Divine (Deep) • Difficulty: Easy

Role & Strengths: Clerics are the sacred shield against darkness, decay, and death. Possessing a deep divine mana pool and solid martial training up to chainmail and shields, they keep adventuring parties alive through catastrophic dungeons and boss encounters.

Primary Attributes:

  • Wisdom (WIS): Core divine stat powering healing dice, spell DC, and maximum mana.
  • Strength (STR): Allows effective bludgeoning strikes with maces in melee.

Equipment Proficiencies:

  • Weapons: Heavy Maces and simple bludgeons.
  • Armors: Cloth, Leather, Studded Cuirass, Chainmail Hauberk.
  • Shields: Buckler (+1 AC), Round Shield (+2 AC), Heater Shield (+2 AC).
Divine Miracle Suite:

Clerics learn essential life-saving miracles: Cure instantly clears deadly marsh and monster poison; Heal and Greater Heal restore massive HP; Protection fortifies party AC; and Resurrect brings fallen companions back into the battle.

✦ THE WIZARD // ARCHMAGE OF DESTRUCTION

Hit Die: d6 • Mana: Arcane (Vast) • Difficulty: Advanced

Role & Strengths: Wizards wield the raw, unbridled forces of the cosmos. Though physically frail with a d6 hit die and limited to cloth tunics, they command the largest mana pool in Astra and can obliterate entire armies with devastating elemental and space-bending sorceries.

Primary Attributes:

  • Intelligence (INT): Direct catalyst for arcane spell damage, spell save DC, and immense mana pools.
  • Dexterity (DEX): Crucial secondary stat to maximize cloth evasion and turn initiative.

Equipment Proficiencies:

  • Weapons: Daggers (finesse).
  • Armors: Cloth Tunics only. Metal armors disrupt spellcasting.
  • Shields: None. Wizards require both hands free to focus astral currents.
The Arcane Grimoire:

Wizards master area-of-effect spells: Fireball (scorching AOE blast), Chain Bolt, Lightning, Sleep, and Time Stop (freezes all enemies for two full rounds). At Level 20, they unlock the pinnacle of astral magic: Armageddon (120 MP), instantly wiping out all foes on the battlefield once per rest.

♪ THE BARD // MAESTRO OF INSPIRATION

Hit Die: d8 • Mana: Bardic • Difficulty: Moderate

Role & Strengths: Bards are charismatic loremasters, enchanters, and support virtuosos. In addition to a broad arsenal of swords, bows, and illusion spells, Bards possess the unique ability to weave The Five Sacred Songs that empower all allies across the entire battlefield.

Primary Attributes:

  • Dexterity & Intelligence: Enhance finesse strikes, bow accuracy, and song mana.
  • Wisdom (WIS): Bolsters song potency and lingering melodies.

Equipment Proficiencies:

  • Weapons: Daggers, Heavy Maces, Hunting Bows, Broadswords, Astral Blades.
  • Armors: Cloth, Leather Armor, Studded Cuirass.
  • Shields: Bucklers (+1 AC).
The Five Songs (Touches All Allies on the Field):
  • Ballad of Mending (Lv 1): Heals all allies immediately and every round (also heals nearby players outside combat).
  • March of Valor (Lv 3): Grants +1 to-hit and +2 damage party-wide (scales at Lv 9 & 15).
  • Aria of the Aegis (Lv 6): Grants +2 AC and direct damage reduction for all companions.
  • Hymn of Vigor (Lv 10): Instantly purges poison and restores mana each round to all allied casters.
  • Stargazer's Anthem (Lv 15): The ultimate song: +2 to-hit, +3 damage, +3 AC, damage warding, and continuous regeneration.

⚙ THE TINKER // CLOCKWORK GUNSMITH

Hit Die: d8 • Mana: Gadgetry • Difficulty: Moderate

Role & Strengths: Tinkers are ingenious engineers and alchemical artillerists. They are the exclusive wielders of firearms in Astra, raining bullets, grenades, and autonomous clockwork constructs upon any monster reckless enough to face them.

Exclusive Firearms Arsenal:

  • Musket Pistol: 1d8 damage, range 3 tiles, one-handed (starts with one).
  • Blunderbuss: 2d6 damage, range 2 tiles, two-handed cone spread hitting adjacent foes for half damage.
  • Musket Rifle: 2d8 damage, range 6 tiles, two-handed sniper rifle with unmatched range.

Equipment & Gunsmith Talents:

  • Armors: Cloth, Leather, Studded Cuirass, Chainmail Hauberk.
  • Gunsmith Bonuses: Gains +1 to hit with firearms at Lv 5, 10, 15, and 20; and +1 damage at Lv 5, 9, 13, and 17.
  • Shields: None (Tinkers keep their off-hand free for firearm loading, powder kegs, and grenade deployment).
Gadgets, Grenades & Autonomous Constructs:

Thrown Munitions: Frag Grenade & Flash-Bang (Lv 1), Smoke Bomb (Lv 4), Glue Bomb (Lv 6), Acid Flask (Lv 7), Incendiary (Lv 9), Cluster Bomb (Lv 10), Tesla Grenade (Lv 13), Gas Grenade (Lv 14), Mortar Barrage (Lv 16), Thermite (Lv 18), Doomsday Device (Lv 20).
Clockwork Allies: Deploy mechanical units that fight independently: Clockwork Turret (Lv 2, stationary turret), Robot Dog (Lv 5, swift multi-biter), Robot Spider (Lv 8, venom injector), and the colossal Steam Golem (Lv 12, frontline heavy bruiser).

COOPERATIVE MULTIPLAYER & THE PERSISTENT REALM

1. A Unified Shared World

Every adventurer explores the exact same continent. The server synchronizes the world clock, day/night transitions, weather fronts (rain, fog, thunderstorms), town markets, and Moongates in real time. Nearby players appear as named avatars; resting adventurers display a sleeping indicator.

2. Chat Channels & Social

Press [T] to open the communications feed. Switch between channels seamlessly:
• /w Name message: Private direct whisper
• /g message: Adventuring group channel
• /l message: Local area channel
• World: Global realm announcements

3. Groups & Shared Encounters

Form parties of up to 4 players via the PLAYERS tab or /invite Name. If a party member is ambushed within 10 tiles, companions are automatically drawn into the tactical combat encounter to fight side by side.

Tactical Combat Reinforcements:

Battles underway in the overworld are visibly marked by clashing blade icons. Any adventurer who steps onto that tile is prompted to join the fray. Combat turns cycle fairly, with a turn timer ensuring smooth progression. XP and gold are awarded in full to all participants, and dropped loot chests can be opened by each hero individually.
